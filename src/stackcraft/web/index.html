    The research rules

    Stackcraft is a simplified, turn-based falling-block game on a 10 × 20 board. There is no gravity timer, hold, wall kick, tuck or T-spin bonus. Clear 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 lines to score 100 / 300 / 500 / 800 points. All players will use the same rules. Model comparisons are coming after verified baseline runs.
